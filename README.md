# The Pilgrim Walk — Poems & Pictures by Timothy Holt

**Live Experience:** [https://drtimotheus.github.io/pilgrim-walk-game/](https://drtimotheus.github.io/pilgrim-walk-game/)

*The Pilgrim Walk* is a 3D WebGL walkable sanctuary through all **44 books of the Bible** in storyline order—from the dawn of Genesis to the New Jerusalem of Revelation—built entirely from Timothy Holt's own **1,677 paintings** and poems.

- **44 Lantern-Lit Book Districts** & **3D Gatehouse Courtyard Hub**
- **1,677 Original Paintings** (1,633 street poem monuments + 44 book covers)
- **176 3D Step-Through Painting Scenes** & **53 Sculpted Hero Relics** (with a 3D Relic Turntable Inspector)
- **Story Walk** (chapter-part Sanctuary Lights & Amen Gates) & **Free Walk** (open exploration across all 44 books)
- **1,633-Day Daily Walk Devotional** (one painting, Scripture, poem, reflection prompt, and candle a day)
- **Selene Voice Narration**, original per-book & per-scene ambient music, and **English / Mandarin (中文)** toggle
- **Pilgrim Progress Code (`PW1-...`)** export/import for carrying saves across browsers, devices, and web addresses

## How to Play & Controls

- **`WASD` / Arrow Keys** *(or **Touch Joystick** on mobile)* — Walk along the Book Streets; drag mouse or touch to look around
- **`[R]`** — Read the painting's Scripture, poem, & reflection prompt, and listen to Selene voice narration
- **`[E]`** — Step inside **176 glowing paintings** (3D Step-Through Scenes), pass through archways, or inspect relics (`[Esc]` or the on-screen **Back to the Street** button returns you to the street)
- **`[F]`** — Kindle hidden **Sanctuary Lights** in Story Walk, or toggle **Free Walk** mode anytime
- **`[J]`** — Open today's **Daily Walk** devotional candle & journal
- **`[P]`** — Save a keepsake **Pilgrim Postcard** of the current painting and poem
